ROI Calculation Formulas

Key Variables

  • Net Upfront Cost = Gross Installation Cost - Subsidies
  • Self-Consumed Solar Energy = Minimum of (Annual Solar Production, Self-Consumption Cap % of Annual Consumption) — cap is adjustable, default 35%
  • Exported Solar Energy = Annual Solar Production - Self-Consumed Solar Energy
  • Annual Savings = (Self-Consumed Solar Energy × Buying Price) + (Exported Solar Energy × Selling Price)

Key Formulas

  • Payback Period = Net Upfront Cost / Annual Savings
  • 25-Year Net Profit = (Annual Savings × 25) - Net Upfront Cost

Assumptions

  • Electricity prices remain constant over 25 years
  • Solar system efficiency remains stable
  • No additional maintenance costs considered
  • Subsidies are applied as a one-time reduction to the gross cost
